Pair Super Productivity with your watch

This app relays over Bluetooth via your phone (Pebble watchapps have no networking of their own), so pairing happens here in the phone browser, not on the watch.

This is the end-to-end encryption password you set in Super Productivity's sync settings (not your login password, if those differ). It never leaves this device: it's used locally to derive the AES key that decrypts your tasks.

SuperSync accounts are authenticated via an emailed magic link or a passkey, not a password, so there's no login form here. Tap "Open SuperSync login" below, sign in there, copy the token it shows you ("Connection Successful - copy this token and paste it in Super Productivity's sync settings"), then come back here and paste it above. It's the same token you'd paste into Super Productivity's own Settings → Sync → SuperSync screen.

Watch display

Shows a bold, underlined project name above each group of tasks on the watch, instead of one flat list.

A task you dictate on the watch (the "Add Task" row, on watches with a microphone) is filed into this project.

Features

Shows the Habits row on the watch and syncs habit data to it. Turn off if you don't use Super Productivity's habit tracking.

Shows the microphone "Add Task" row on watches with a microphone. Watches without a microphone never show this row regardless of this setting.

Experimental, for the Pebble Time 2: swipe to scroll, tap a row to toggle/open it, long-press for the press-and-hold action, swipe right to go back. The physical buttons keep working too. Off by default - the current Time 2 touch firmware misreads taps near the screen edges. No effect on watches without a touchscreen.
